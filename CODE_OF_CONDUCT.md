# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Helpful behavior
includes empathy, respect for different experience levels, constructive feedback,
responsible handling of private device data, and accepting correction when
compatibility evidence is incomplete.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language,
personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, encouraging
unsafe public exposure of household devices, or distributing proprietary vendor
software through this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that violate these standards. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the same repository-host channel
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will respect the privacy and
safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement decisions should explain the behavior and its impact, not
debate a person's identity or worth.
